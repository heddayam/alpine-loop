# Washington mainland north edge

Reviewed 2026-09-25. The Washington provider extent now uses the [International Boundary Commission (IBC) digital boundary v1.3](https://www.internationalboundarycommission.org/en/maps-coordinates/coordinates.php) along its mainland north edge. The archived [v1.3 shapefile](https://www.internationalboundarycommission.org/uploads/shapefile/us-canada-boundary-v1-3.zip) has SHA-256 `eb327459528b87cbc27e55ccc6bfd6982562c75559823a00b6dc50c04abcaab1`; its `.shp` has SHA-256 `77db98da135852843be4cdc4e3d23319a7113f8d77e1b1b963b2da63be2ef022`. The IBC says this NAD83 monument-derived file is **for mapping only** and does not legally define the boundary. No legal access conclusion follows from this geometry.

The original Geofabrik Washington polygon remains the basis for the coast, east, and south edges. The replacement uses IBC records 6, 2, and 10 between the western mainland 49th-parallel point `[-123.090699546, 49.002058219]` and its intersection with the Geofabrik eastern edge `[-117.025231978, 48.999225501]`. The western connection starts at `[-123.090699546, 49.006865535]` on the original provider edge. Maritime/coastal border geography west of this connection is outside this mainland correction. The provider polygon was downloaded 2026-09-24; its source URL, SHA-256, and historical-extract limitation remain in the GeoJSON properties. This is a reviewed geographic support extent, not proof that trails or DEM are complete up to the border.

The IBC line at longitude -121.774 is latitude 48.997541994. All 40 nodes of pinned OSM way/1186146299 (`BR717`) are north of the interpolated IBC line by about 14–708 m. Its closest node, `node/11017326772` at `[-121.7736891, 48.99766]`, is 13.7 m north. BR717 is therefore outside Washington in this mapping, explaining why the USGS n49w122 elevation tile is nodata there. In contrast, pinned OSM `node/677621543` on Chilliwack River Trail `way/53658218` at `[-121.4077738, 48.9998624]` is south of the IBC line (roughly 10 m) and remains inside source coverage. The North Cascades pack boundary and DEM quality near that trail require separate review; changing source coverage alone does not imply that trail is installed.

For Cascades requests, the recipe's upper latitude is 49.01 so it can include US land north of the geometric 49th parallel; the IBC source geometry still determines which part is supported. The app's matching collection envelope also needs 49.01. The offline coverage test fixes the BR717 and Chilliwack classifications and confirms interior West Cady/Pilchuck points remain covered.
